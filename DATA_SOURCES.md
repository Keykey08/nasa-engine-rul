# Data source
**CMAPSS Jet Engine Simulated Data**, NASA Prognostics Center of Excellence. Official download: https://data.nasa.gov/docs/legacy/CMAPSSData.zip (catalog: https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/cmapss-jet-engine-simulated-data). US government public data.

Saxena, A., Goebel, K., Simon, D. and Eklund, N. (2008) 'Damage Propagation Modeling for Aircraft Engine Run-to-Failure Simulation', Proceedings of the 1st International Conference on Prognostics and Health Management (PHM08), Denver, CO.
